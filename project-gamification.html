01 / The problem

What needed to change.

GM wanted to modernize employee engagement and internal learning through a centralized gamification platform for events, resources, leaderboards, and interactive company initiatives.

02 / My contribution

Where I made a difference.

  • Selected as the lead UX/UI designer and designed the full platform from concept to production.
  • Created wireframes, user flows, prototypes, and high-fidelity Figma designs.
  • Led frontend implementation while collaborating with backend and database teams on SQL integration.

03 / Product capabilities

What the product supported.

  • Employee profiles
  • Competitive leaderboards
  • Video-based learning
  • Event tracking
  • Interactive educational content

04 / Outcome

Why it mattered.

Modernized employee engagement and learning by creating a more interactive, accessible environment for internal participation and education.
